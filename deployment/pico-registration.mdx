A Pico unit joins the hub on its own — you do not register it by hand. This page explains how the unit appears, how to confirm it, and what to check when it does not.

## How a unit appears

When a flashed Pico unit boots, it connects to your Wi-Fi network and announces itself to the hub at `POST /v1/pico-units/announce`, sending its shared secret in the `X-Pico-Secret` header.

The hub matches the announcement by the unit's `handle` — the `device_name` from the unit's `config.json` — and upserts the unit into its database. From then on the hub polls the unit every 60 seconds for temperature and humidity readings. You do not flash, configure, or wire anything in this step — that is covered on the [Pico firmware](/hardware/pico-firmware) and [Wiring](/hardware/wiring) pages.

## Confirm the unit is registered

Power on the Pico. It connects to Wi-Fi and announces itself, and it appears in the dashboard within a minute or two.

Open the dashboard at `http://<hub-ip>:3000` and look at the monitoring view, which shows a card for each registered unit. The new unit's card appears with its `handle`, and its temperature and humidity readings start filling in after the hub's first poll — allow up to 60 seconds for the first reading.

## If the unit does not appear

Work through these checks in order:

**The shared secret.** The `hub_secret` in the unit's `config.json` must exactly match the hub's `PICO_ANNOUNCE_SECRET`. A mismatch means the hub rejects the announcement and the unit never appears.

**The hub address.** The `hub_url` in `config.json` must point at the hub's real address, including the path — `http://<hub-ip>:3000/v1/pico-units/announce`. If the hub's IP has changed, the unit is announcing to the wrong place.

**The unit is on the network.** If the unit never reaches your Wi-Fi network, it cannot announce. The unit's onboard LED tells you where it is stuck — Wi-Fi not connected, Wi-Fi up but the hub unreachable, or fully operational. Compare the LED against the [Status indicators](/hardware/status-indicators) page. To change the Wi-Fi credentials, see [Provisioning](/hardware/provisioning).

You can register as many units as you need — each one announces itself the same way, and each controls its own grow space.

This is step 3 of a grow. Once a batch is running, two screens show you how it is doing: the **Dashboard** (`/`), which gives you an at-a-glance view of everything, and the **Readings** page, which holds the historical charts.

## The Dashboard's warnings

The Dashboard opens with a warnings banner when something needs your attention. It groups warnings into four kinds:

| Warning | What it means |
|---|---|
| **A unit is unreachable** | The server cannot reach the unit, so it is offline or off the network. The banner lists the unit by name. |
| **A unit is deviating** | The unit is reporting normally, but its temperature or humidity has drifted outside the batch's target. The banner counts these units. |
| **A unit is degraded** | The unit is reachable but reporting errors, such as repeated failed reads. |
| **A unit has no readings** | The server can reach the unit, but its sensor is not returning a temperature or humidity value. |

Click a warning to jump to that unit's page.

## The unit cards

Below the warnings, one card per unit shows:

- **Health** — healthy, degraded, offline, or unmonitored.
- **Last temperature and humidity** — the most recent reading.
- **A firmware-compatibility badge** — shown when the unit's firmware reports a contract the server flags as incompatible.
- **A control-loop chip** — whether the unit's control loop is currently enabled.
- **The active batch, with days left** — the grow running on that unit and how long it has to run.
- **Last seen** — how long ago the unit last reported, as a relative age.

## The batch and recipe widgets

Below the cards, the Dashboard summarises the rest:

- **Active batches** — the grows currently running.
- **Approaching completion** — batches nearing their finish date.
- **Recently finished** — batches that just ended.
- **Most-used recipes** — the recipes you reach for most often.

A **stats row** at the top gives the totals at a glance: units (and how many are paused), active batches, total batches, and total readings.

## The Readings page

Click **Readings** in the sidebar for the full history of a unit. The page has four controls:

- **Unit select** — an in-page dropdown to choose which unit's readings to show.
- **Time window** — the period to show, for example the last hour.
- **Display points** — how many points the charts plot, or **Auto**.
- **Export** — download the readings as a CSV file.

### The three chart tabs

| Tab | What it plots |
|---|---|
| **Temperature & humidity** | Temperature and humidity over time, with the batch's targets drawn against them |
| **Devices** | Whether the humidifier, fan and heater were on or off |
| **Control loop** | Whether the control loop was enabled or disabled |

The batch detail screen shows the same three tabs, but clamped to that batch's dates.

## Reading what you see

**What healthy looks like:** the unit's health is **healthy**, there are no warnings, and temperature and humidity track close to the targets — small, short dips and rises either side of the target line are normal, because that is the control loop doing its job.

**What drift is worth acting on:** a reading that stays away from the target for an extended stretch, or a warning that keeps returning, is worth investigating. Check the unit first — power, sensor, and whether the devices are actually switching — before changing targets.

**How the control loop responds to the targets:** the loop on the Pico reads the sensor and switches the heater, fan and humidifier to bring conditions back toward the targets you set on the batch. How far conditions may drift before it acts — the hysteresis band — is explained in [Hysteresis control](/configuration/hysteresis-control).

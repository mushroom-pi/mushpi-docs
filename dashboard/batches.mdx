A batch in Mushroom Pi represents a single cultivation run — the full arc from inoculation through colonisation and fruiting all the way to harvest. Every grow you do gets its own batch record, giving you a permanent, searchable history of what species you grew, when you started, which environmental recipe you followed, and which Pico unit was managing the space. Over time your batch history becomes a practical log that helps you repeat successful grows and understand why others fell short.

## What a batch contains

Each batch stores the following information:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Species / strain name** | A free-text label for what you are growing (e.g. "Pearl Oyster — WC-03"). |
| **Start date** | The date the batch was inoculated or spawned. |
| **Notes** | Any free-form observations — substrate formula, contamination events, flush notes. |
| **Linked recipe** | An optional reference to a saved recipe that defines the temperature and humidity targets for this batch. |
| **Pico unit** | The specific unit that controls the grow space for this batch. |

## Create a new batch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Batches">
    Open the Mushroom Pi dashboard in your browser and click **Batches** in the left-hand navigation sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Batch">
    Click the **New Batch** button in the top-right corner of the Batches page. A creation form opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in species, start date, and notes">
    Enter the species or strain name in the **Species** field. Set the **Start Date** to the date you inoculated or spawned your substrate. Add any relevant notes in the **Notes** field — this is optional but recommended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link a recipe (optional)">
    If you have already created a recipe for this species, select it from the **Recipe** dropdown. The targets defined in that recipe (temperature, humidity, hysteresis band) will be applied to the control loop for this batch's unit. You can leave this blank and link a recipe later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Pico unit">
    Choose the Pico unit that is managing the grow space for this batch from the **Unit** dropdown. Each batch is associated with exactly one unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The batch is now active and appears in your batch list. The dashboard will track environmental readings from the selected unit against this batch going forward.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing batch history

All completed and active batches are listed on the Batches page. The list shows the species name, start date, linked recipe, and associated unit for each batch at a glance. Click any batch to open its detail view, where you can:

- Review the full notes history.
- See which recipe was applied and when.
- Browse environmental data recorded during the batch's lifetime.
- Mark the batch as complete once you have finished harvesting.

Completed batches are kept permanently, giving you a historical record you can refer back to when planning future grows.

<Tip>
  Link a recipe to your batch before you start the run. When a recipe is attached, the control loop on the Pico uses that recipe's temperature and humidity targets automatically — you do not need to dial in thresholds manually for each new grow. If you grow the same species repeatedly, a single saved recipe is all you need.
</Tip>

Mushroom Pi puts automated environmental control within reach of every home cultivator. A Raspberry Pi Pico 2W reads your grow space's temperature and humidity, drives humidifier, fan, and heater relays through a hysteresis control loop, and reports back to a self-hosted server you run on your own hardware — all visible and configurable from a web dashboard in your browser.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Flash the firmware">
    Copy the MicroPython firmware onto your Raspberry Pi Pico 2W and set your Wi-Fi credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the hub">
    Clone `mushpi-ops` and run `docker compose up -d` — one container serves the backend and dashboard on port 3000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Navigate to `http://<hub-ip>:3000` in your browser and register your Pico unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start growing">
    Create a batch, set your target temperature and humidity ranges, and let Mushroom Pi manage the environment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

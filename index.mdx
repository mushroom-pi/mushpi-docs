Mushroom Pi puts automated environmental control within reach of every home cultivator. A Raspberry Pi Pico 2W reads your grow space's temperature and humidity, drives humidifier, fan, and heater relays through a hysteresis control loop, and reports back to a self-hosted server you run on a Raspberry Pi 3 — all visible and configurable from a web dashboard in your browser.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Flash the firmware">
    Copy the MicroPython firmware onto your Raspberry Pi Pico 2W and configure it with your Wi-Fi credentials and the backend's address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the server">
    Pull the `ghcr.io/mushroom-pi/mushpi` container and run it on your Raspberry Pi 3 — the backend and dashboard ship together on port 3000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the dashboard">
    Navigate to `http://<your-pi-ip>:3000` in your browser, register your Pico unit, and set your target temperature and humidity ranges.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start growing">
    Create a batch, assign a recipe, and let Mushroom Pi manage the environment while you watch the charts update in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

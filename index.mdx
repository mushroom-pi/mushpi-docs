Mushroom Pi puts automated environmental control within reach of every home cultivator. A Raspberry Pi Pico 2W reads your grow space's temperature and humidity and drives humidifier, fan, and heater relays through a hysteresis control loop — entirely on its own, with no server and no internet. Add a self-hosted hub when you want a dashboard, reading history, and batches on top.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Flash the firmware">
    Copy the MicroPython firmware onto your Raspberry Pi Pico 2W and set your Wi-Fi credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power up and use the unit">
    Open the unit's own page to read the climate and set targets — no hub, no internet required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the hub dashboard (optional)">
    Clone `mushpi-hub` and run `docker compose up -d` to add a dashboard, history and batches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start growing">
    Set your target temperature and humidity and let the unit hold them. If you added the hub, create a batch there to track the run.
  </Step>
</Steps>
